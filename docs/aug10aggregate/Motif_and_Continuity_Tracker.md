# Motif & Continuity Tracker
*Reconciled to the revised cycle · August 2026*

## Purpose
Keep every recurring image, object, beat, and character note consistent across the seven tales so the cycle reads as one continuous oral tradition. Consult before drafting or revising anything.

---

## Core Motifs

### 1. The Living Doll (Кукла, которая слушала)
- Fed with a crumb of bread and a drop of clean water. Never varies.
- Warms with true listening, presence, giving, and truth spoken near it.
- Cools at falsehood — an ordinary passing cool, "a change in the wind."
- **The measured cold is different and has a bottom to it.** It begins in Tale 3 (fed, and will not warm), deepens through Tale 4, and **releases into ordinary wood at the mother's death** — not warmth, not comfort, just plain wood with nothing more to say.
- Tale 5: goes cold in her pocket at the moment of the lie. She feels it, knows what it means, and does not take the words back.
- Tales 6–7: goes still or cool when a bundle is packed or a road opens; warms whenever the flame is actually offered, confirming the truth of the giving.
- Speaks or is felt only by Vasilisa. Kept under the pillow or near the beautiful corner.

### 2. Unrecognized Light — **varied by design**
The three old "approved phrasings" are retired as a template. Each instance is now different, and two are not praise at all. Current canon:

| Tale | Speaker | Instance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the friend of the heart | "You look at things the way the icon looks — quiet, but nothing is hidden from you." |
| 1 | the mother | "There is a steady light in you, my girl. Do you feel it?" |
| 2 | two women on the frost path | "He spoke for the better part of an hour and I believed every word of it." / "And she asked one question, and I didn't." |
| 3 | a woman by the lampada | "The light in this house has not gone out." *(about the house, not her)* |
| 4 | a traveller's older woman | "Where did you learn to be that quiet with a frightened thing?" |
| 5 | the nurse | "You bring a different light into these rooms. I don't know where you keep it." |
| 5 | the clear-eyed woman | "the one who comes out" → **"Nika."** |
| 6 | — | none; the motif rests |
| 7 | Marek | "You keep a fire for people who never once asked where you got the wood." |

Vasilisa turns each outward or back to the work until Tale 5.

### 3. Black Host / Iron Birds / Sky-Thunder
Consistent imagery only; never modern war language, never political. The High Place is the old ground behind the izba where true words were once spoken under open sky — it draws the iron birds and the sky-thunder, which is what makes the house a target. Tale 3 primary; residual memory afterward.

### 4. Living Water
Sought four days east, under a scarred white birch the iron birds circle but have not poisoned. Eases the body and lengthens the days. **Cannot turn back what is already measured.** Buys twenty-one days.

### 5. Solitary Knowledge of the Mother's Measured Time
- Begins Tale 3 as action: the doll is fed and will not warm.
- Carried alone through Tale 4; the white bird on the threshold at the moment of death.
- **Resolved in Tale 4:** the mother reveals she knew — *"Did you think I couldn't feel you not saying it? It made a draught in the room"* — and names the silence as the last of the housework, done right.
- She protects both parents; the hurt rides beside the flame, never as self-pity.

### 6. Father's Thinning Presence
Early warmth and the private working-hands lessons (Tale 1); journeys lengthen, laughter lessens; becomes a road he does not come back along. Never villainous. His timber and grain-memory become the Quiet Walker's body — the lessons outlive the man.

### 7. Orthodox Household Texture
Krasny ugol, icon of the Mother of God, lampada, crossing oneself before journeys and hard weighing, the mother teaching simple prayers beside the old songs. Lived, never preached. Continues into the foreign kingdom as a red corner "no larger than a shelf, yet clean."

### 8. Continuous Helping-Others Thread
Natural and instinctive across all seven. Continues inside the Golden Cage and through the residual years. Never framed as self-sacrifice for its own sake.

### 9. Gray River / Crossing
The great border that changes the walker. No bridge. The Walker becomes a living raft, holds against the current at midway, and returns its strength into the small doll and the river on the far bank. She arrives alone with the doll and the old songs.

### 10. Golden Cage / Stone-Hearted Boyar
Gold that does not warm, stone that does not answer, being unseen inside her own house. She keeps giving. Tale 5 primary; after-echo through 6–7.

### 11. Soft Recognition of Nika (Tale 5)
Spoken by a clear-eyed woman at the outer door who has been steadied by her hands. The doll grows warmer than it ever has. She sees the flame she has carried — **including that it is beautiful.** The road branches; the choice is hers as Nika.

### 12. The Quiet Walker / Threshold One (Тихий Ходок) — **updated**
- Larger kin to the doll: wood carrying the father's grain-memory, and threshold/river clay. Simple of face, larger than a child, smaller than a man.
- **Origin:** formed during the patient research after the empty house. Materials and quiet guidance provided; the shaping, feeding, listening, and completion are entirely hers. She midwifed it and never feared it.
- **Creation-flaw — now a scene, not a report.** At the heart-place a true word is wanted. She cannot say the true reason for the road and says instead: *"There is no one left who would miss me, and nothing in me that anyone would want to keep."* The joint closes cold, with a dull knock instead of a clean note. This directly violates the mother's Tale 1 warning.
- **Function on the road:** path-knower, burden-bearer, living raft. Nothing on the journey requires a heart, so nothing shows.
- **Residual transformation:** first small silences in Tale 6 (the threshold stops leaning; the bread goes untouched; her voice sounds wrong in a room). Full in Tale 7 — it empties rooms, turns feet aside, stands in doorways, does not answer.
- **It is not malicious.** It has one instruction about her and is keeping it with terrible faithfulness. This is essential and must not drift toward villainy.
- **Undone by truth, not by force.** In Tale 7 she names the lie and replaces it. Never called golem or automaton.

### 13. Residual Cage / Safety of the Known Walls / The Inward Bar
- After the naming, the after-echo of the cage continues; the cost is now self-supplied.
- After the first shut gate, an inward sting takes root: any thought of freedom or small happiness is closed on at once, "as if she herself had already locked the gate." She knows it is wrong; knowing makes no difference; the sting arrives first.
- The silent Walker learns this bar "the way a dog learns a whistle" and afterward mostly only has to be silent.
- **Approved renderings — image only, never clinical:** the sting; the coin in the pocket; the bar she had learned to set against herself; the threshold heavier under one foot; a song that catches; «укол»; «засов, который она сама себе поставила»; «сама наперёд заперла ворота».
- **Forbidden on the page (both languages):** self-punishment, isolation, communication, autonomy, trauma, internalized cost; «самонаказание», «изоляция», «общение», «автономия».

### 14. Threat that is Really a Lifeline
Appears first as danger or enforced silence; met with the quiet flame and the living link, it proves to be the opening of the path. Resolved by her own agency.

### 15. The Stranger and the Living Link (Tale 7) — **updated**
- **Marek** — a name he gives after a half-breath's pause, which she notices and never resolves. The ambiguity is dramatized by that pause, not stated.
- Recently out of a prison of stone and long silence. Thin, road-worn, boots finished. Holds bread in both hands before eating. Will not have the door closed: *"Leave it open. I'll pay for the draught."*
- His key line on what a locked place leaves behind: the quiet comes with you, in your pocket, and you find it reaching for a coin.
- He names her inward bar without pity — the only reason she can hear it.
- Light presence; catalyst and living mirror only. **He is not a rescuer.** In Tale 7 she does not leave *for* him.
- After he goes: mind to mind, heart to heart. Residual warmth in the doll that is not only its own; a second voice keeping the low part in the old songs; a steadiness that answers across distance. The silence cannot fully stop it — "that non-absoluteness was new."

## Character Continuity

**Brother** — scenery only. A jay's feather presented as a great matter, kept for years in the chest with the winter cloth. Loved, and rarely in the room. Leaves for a distant land around the season she leaves the border. No further weight.

**Lifelong Friend of the Heart (Anya-modeled)** — girlhood companion, mutual helping, lighter and more changeable in temperament, without judgment. Light presence in Tales 1–2. **Recalled in Tale 5** as "a friend somewhere west of here who had held half her chores since they were seven years old" — one of the ropes with the other end cut.

**Minor figures now fixed in canon:**
- *Aleksei, Katya, old Marfa* (Tale 2)
- *the neighbour's boy* who counts bricks to nine (Tale 3)
- *the old woman with the fallen roof*, who brings word of the spring (Tales 3–4)
- *the lost child* with cut feet, and the older travelling woman who asks where she learned that quiet (Tale 4)
- *the nurse with the burn-scarred wrist*, eleven years in the house (Tales 5–7)
- *the clear-eyed woman at the low gate* who says "Nika" (Tale 5)
- *the kitchen girl with the chipped tooth*, who counts ceiling boards (Tale 6)
- *Marek* (Tale 7)

## Structural Notes
- The inventory opening (doll fed / lampada / mother by stove / father's place empty) is retired as a tale-opening device. It survives only where it earns its place mid-scene.
- Tale 5 still carries more material than any other tale even with the death moved out. Splitting it remains the one open structural option.
