# World, Tone, Context & Vocabulary Guide
*Reconciled to the revised cycle · August 2026*

## Setting
The long-ago of the skazki. A border village at the edge of forest and steppe → the lands under the Black Host → the Gray River → a foreign kingdom of strange customs → the residual years of the known walls and the open threshold beyond. Time is the timeless "long ago," never historical.

## Tone
Luminous, warm, slightly melancholy, oral cadence. Beauty alongside hardship. Resilience quiet and continuous. The voice is that of the Domovoi of the old border houses, or the women who keep the mother's spindle and tend the lampada.

**Concrete over abstract, always.** The single most common failure in this cycle has been naming a state instead of showing an object. A house does not experience isolation; the bread stays on the step until the birds find it.

## Orthodox Texture (lived, never preached)
The krasny ugol with icon and lampada; crossing oneself before a journey or hard weighing; the mother teaching the old songs and a few simple prayers; the quiet sense that the flame is held under a higher care; distant bells and icon-light as atmosphere. Provision of means meets her full agency; the higher care is never didactic.

## Preferred Vocabulary & Imagery
- тихий огонь / ровный свет — quiet fire / steady light
- кукла, которая слушала — the doll that listened
- красный угол, лампада, икона, домовой
- живая вода — Living Water
- чёрное воинство / железные птицы / гром с неба
- серая река — Gray River
- золотая клетка — Golden Cage
- порог, печь, берёзовый свет
- «слушать землю» — to listen to the land
- working hands / mending what is broken; the grain that is asked what it wants to become; the clean note of a thing made whole
- the long road that changes the walker
- Quiet Walker / Threshold One / the wooden companion that rose / порождённое порогом — never "golem," never "automaton"

## Forbidden
No modern, clinical, political, or contemporary language. No direct equivalents of real events. Everything must feel as if it has always belonged to the old tales.

**Never, in either language, on the page:**

| Do not write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| isolation, being isolated | the house went quiet wherever she was; the rooms emptied; «дом умолк»; «пустота»; «глухота» |
| communication stopped | no one answered; the bread stayed on the step; feet turned off into doorways before they reached her; «слова кончились»; «никто не отзывался» |
| autonomy | what little was hers to order; her hours were counted, her step watched; «воли у неё и прежде было мало, а стало и того меньше» |
| self-punishment | the sting; the bar she had learned to set against herself; as if she had already locked the gate herself; «укол»; «засов, который она сама себе поставила» |
| trauma, internalized, psychological | — no substitute; rewrite the sentence as an image |

Russian is the harder case: «автономия», «общение», «изоляция», «самонаказание» are newspaper words and clang far louder in folk register than their English equivalents. Russian has deep native register available for every one of these ideas — use it.

## Russian Composition Notes
- Compose in Russian; do not track English clause order. Translationese shows up first as over-long subordinate chains and stacked participles.
- Dialogue takes the em-dash on a new line, never quotation marks.
- Particles and inversion carry the oral voice: уж, ведь, знай себе, было, покуда, эдак.
- Folk lexis over neutral: «изба», «горница», «поленница», «оглобля», «мешковина», «нижняя ступень».
- Watch agreement on predicative short forms — «она знала, что это не по правде», not «что это неправедный».

## Residual-Cage Layer (Tales 6–7)
The living doll grows still or cool at the open gate; a song catches unfinished in the throat; the threshold heavier under one foot; a needed task rises under her own feet; the higher care felt in her own heart asking her to remain one more season; the presence that no longer leans toward her door; her own voice sounding wrong in the room, "the way a voice goes in an empty church"; the quiet that comes with you in your pocket and is there when you reach for a coin.
