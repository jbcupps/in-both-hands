# CHANGELOG — Revised Cycle, August 2026

Complete record of every change from the July 2026 texts to this package.

## Structural

**The mother's death moved from Tale 5 to Tale 4.** It had been a subordinate clause — *"after the measured time of the mother had ended"* — after four tales of accumulation. It is now the closing movement of Tale 4: the Living Water buys twenty-one days, the mother reveals she had known all along and let her daughter keep the silence, and the doll's measured cold releases into ordinary wood at the moment of death. Tale 5 now opens in aftermath rather than report.

**The creation-flaw became a scene.** The lie that leaves the Quiet Walker without a true heart had happened offstage in a subordinate clause, twice. It now occupies its own scene on the swept floor of the empty house, with canonical spoken wording, and the doll goes cold in her pocket as she says it.

**The lie was planted and paid.** Tale 1 now carries the mother's warning — *do not lie to it; it takes what it is given and it keeps it.* Tale 7's threshold speech answers it directly. The Walker's silence is reframed throughout as faithfulness to one instruction rather than malice.

**Inventory openings retired.** Tales 3, 4, 6 and 7 all opened with the same sweep (doll fed / crumb / clean water / lampada / mother by stove / father's place empty). All four now open on action or image.

## Tale 7 — Ending and Corrections

The ending was revised by the author to leave the Choice open: she does not take the road that day, the name is claimed in the standing itself, the bars are met and are no longer absolute, and the flame is offered to the road if she takes it and to the walls if she remains. This restores the project's original Final Agency intent and is preserved as written.

Four mechanical corrections were applied on 2026-08-10, none touching that decision:

1. Removed a duplicated paragraph pair — *"The bar she had set against herself…"* and *"And it met the true sentence…"* — which appeared twice with a stray divider between them.
2. Removed a duplicated ritual beat (feeding the doll, trimming the lampada, the song, the sign of the cross), which occurred both before and after the confrontation.
3. Changed *"one last crumb… inside the house of gold and stone"* to the ordinary feeding, since "last" only holds if she is leaving.
4. Fixed an attribution error in the threshold speech: *"My mother put you in my hands"* was addressed to the Walker, but the mother gave her the **doll**. Now *"My mother put your kin in my hands and told me never to lie to her"* — which also strengthens the link back to Tale 1.

## Russian

All seven Russian texts were composed fresh from the revised English rather than patched, because the old files were pre-revision throughout and diverged from the English at the climax.

**Clinical vocabulary purged.** The July texts contained seventeen instances of «автономия», «общение», «изоляция» and «самонаказание» in Tale 7 alone — the last of which the project's own tone guide forbids by name. A sweep of this package returns zero across all seven files. Replacements are folk throughout: «дом умолк», «укол», «засов, который она сама себе поставила», «воли у неё и прежде было мало».

**Grammar.** The ungrammatical «Она знала, что это неправедный» is gone; the idea is now carried by «не по правде».

**Register.** Composed in Russian rather than tracked clause-by-clause from the English, so word order, particles and aspect are native. Dialogue uses em-dashes on new lines.

The doll/Walker attribution fix was made in the Russian first and carried back into the English.

## Open Items

- **Tale 5 length.** Even with the death moved out, it carries the making, the crossing, the binding, the cage years and the naming. Splitting it is the one remaining structural option and would break the seven-tale lock.
- **Tale 7's ending.** *"She did not choose either of them that day"* sits somewhat against a tale titled *The Choice* built on three attempts — more audibly in Russian, where the title word appears in the sentence. If ever addressed, the minimal fix is one line naming the truth-telling as the choosing. Nothing else would need to move.
