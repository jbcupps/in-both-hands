# The Quiet Flame of Vasilisa
## *(How the Border Girl Became Nika)* — Project Plan
*Reconciled to the revised cycle · August 2026*

The seven-tale structure is locked. The cycle is complete in English and Russian. All texts below are canonical; the support documents in this package have been reconciled to them.

## Core Through-Line
Vasilisa is given a quiet, inherited flame — the mother's living doll and the old songs, the practical lessons of working hands from a father who once sang and danced by the stove, the simple prayers and icon-light of the household. Through successive losses she never lets that flame go out and never stops offering it.

One of her heaviest burdens is solitary knowledge: she alone is shown that her mother's remaining time is already measured. She protects both parents from it while giving full care and walking the dangerous road for a water that can only ease.

Others see a quiet beauty and clarity in her that she does not yet claim. The name Nika is softly recognized at the end of Tale 5. The residual safety of the known walls is lived in Tale 6. In Tale 7 she frees herself by telling the truth to what she made, claims the name fully, and the road is left open in her hands. She has always been Nika.

## The Seven Tales

**1. The Quiet Flame of Vasilisa / Тихое пламя Василисы**
Childhood in the border village. The warm household; the father's private working-hands hours; the friend of the heart. The mother presses the living doll into her hands, teaches the old songs and the simple prayers — **and warns her never to lie to it, because it keeps what it is given.**
*Carries: luminous roots, the mother's gift and warning, the father's early warmth, the first seeds of helping, the two origin phrasings of the light-motif.*
**Status: Revised & locked (EN + RU), August 2026.**

**2. The Seeker of True Shadows / Искательница истинных теней**
One sustained scene: the suitor Aleksei, the gathering at Marfa's, the single question that changes the room. Opens on his arrival rather than on exposition.
*Carries: clear sight into hidden hearts, continued quiet helping, and — new — the cost of that sight, a thin loneliness.*
**Status: Revised & locked (EN + RU), August 2026.**

**3. When the Black Host Came and the High Place Burned / Когда пришло Чёрное Воинство и сгорело Высокое Место**
The Host rides out. Her vocation closes across two failed gatherings. The High Place draws the sky-thunder; she keeps the hearth through the night. **The measured knowing begins as action: she feeds the doll and it will not warm.**
*Carries: the collapse of the life she had built, the home that becomes a target, the beginning of solitary knowing.*
**Status: Revised & locked (EN + RU), August 2026.**

**4. The Road of the Living Water / Дорога живой воды**
The old woman's account of the spring; four days east through the burned country; the ditch and the iron birds; the lost child. The water eases and buys twenty-one days. **The mother dies onstage at the close** — she had known all along — and the doll's measured cold releases into ordinary wood.
*Carries: protective love, the perilous road, the solitary burden, and its resolution.*
**Status: Revised & locked (EN + RU), August 2026.** *Mother's death moved here from Tale 5.*

**5. The Golden Cage and the Name She Would Not Claim / Золотая клетка и имя, которое она не хотела назвать**
The empty house; the patient research; the making of the Quiet Walker. **The creation-flaw is a scene:** at the heart-place she cannot say the true reason for the road and says instead that no one would miss her and there is nothing in her worth keeping. The joint closes cold. Then the crossing, the binding, the cage, the fever-watch, and the naming: *Nika.*
*Carries: the Walker's formation and flaw, the Gray River, the loveless binding, continued giving, the soft recognition — including that the flame is beautiful.*
**Status: Revised & locked (EN + RU), August 2026.**

**6. The Struggle and the Safety of the Cage / Борьба и безопасность клетки**
The bundle under the bed. The first small silences: the threshold stops leaning, the bread goes untouched, her own voice sounds wrong in the room. Two openings, two costs supplied by her own care and habit. She sees the pattern once and looks away.
*Carries: residual self-binding after recognition, the latent flaw beginning to show, continuous offering, the road left open.*
**Status: Revised & locked (EN + RU), August 2026.**

**7. The Choice / Выбор**
Three attempts. The transformed Walker empties rooms, turns feet aside, stands in doorways and will not answer — **not from malice but from one instruction faithfully kept.** The inward bar takes root after the first shut gate. Marek shares the silence while only passing; afterward they speak mind to mind, heart to heart. At the threshold she names the lie and speaks the true sentence in its place, and the bar meets it and cannot hold. **The road is left open:** she claims the name in the standing itself, and the choosing of the road remains hers.
*Carries: three attempts, silence as enforcement, the inward bar, the shared cold, the living link, the truth-telling that frees, the full claiming of Nika, the open road.*
**Status: Revised & locked (EN + RU), August 2026.** *Ending revised to keep the Choice open; four mechanical corrections applied 2026-08-10.*

## Framing, Tone & Texture
Told on the longest nights by the Domovoi of the old border houses, or by women who keep the mother's spindle and tend the lampada. Luminous, warm, slightly melancholy, oral cadence, beauty held alongside hardship. Soft Orthodox presence woven throughout as lived household reality, sitting naturally beside the Domovoi, the doll, and the older layer of the land.

## Working Rules
1. Consult the Motif & Continuity Tracker before drafting or revising anything.
2. Never open a tale with the inventory sweep (doll fed / lampada / mother by stove / father's place empty).
3. Never reuse a light-motif phrasing verbatim across tales — see the tracker's table.
4. Never name a psychological state; show the object, the gesture, or the room.
5. The Russian is composed, not translated. See the composition notes in the World & Tone guide.

## Open Items
- Tale 5 still carries more material than any other tale. Splitting it is the one remaining structural option and would require breaking the seven-tale lock.
- Tale 7's ending leaves the road unchosen. If the deflation ever needs addressing, the minimal fix is a single line naming the truth-telling as the choosing; nothing else would move.
